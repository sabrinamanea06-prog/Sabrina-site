01 / About Me

Rooted in Silicon Valley.
Shaped by the world.

I'm Sabrina Manea. Born and raised in Silicon Valley, I've always believed that the best opportunities come from curiosity, hard work, and the courage to take an unconventional path.

At 16, I moved to Barcelona on my own to pursue competitive tennis. Training and competing abroad taught me far more than how to perform under pressure. It taught me discipline, resilience, independence, and the value of showing up fully for something I believe in. Those lessons continue to shape the way I work: I prepare carefully, stay determined when challenges arise, and never lose sight of the goal.

Living in Spain also broadened my perspective on people, cultures, and what makes a place feel like home. I speak English and Spanish fluently, Romanian at an advanced level, and intermediate French. Being able to connect across languages and backgrounds helps me listen more closely, communicate thoughtfully, and make clients feel understood throughout an important life decision.

My generation brings a different lens to real estate. I grew up with technology, understand the power of digital storytelling, and bring fresh energy and creative marketing instincts to a constantly evolving market. As an economics student and entrepreneur, I pair that perspective with research, attention to detail, and a practical understanding of how to present opportunities clearly.

And while my experiences have taken me across the world, Silicon Valley has always been home. I know the communities, the character of the neighborhoods, and the pace of life here from firsthand experience. As a licensed California real estate salesperson with The Boyenga Team at Compass, my priority is simple: listen first, give honest guidance, work relentlessly, and do what's best for my clients—even when that means recommending patience over a quick decision.

For me, real estate isn't about pushing a transaction. It's about earning trust and helping people make a move that's right for them.
